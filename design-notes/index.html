Information architecture / 信息架构

Home → Processes → Products → Applications → Knowledge → About → Contact. Services and company news remain accessible from the footer. Process, product and article detail pages cross-link through their shared topic.

GEO foundations implemented / 已实现

  • Static HTML for every page: important content is readable without executing JavaScript.
  • Stable descriptive URLs, page-specific titles and descriptions, canonical tags, a sitemap and breadcrumbs.
  • Product capability tables, short direct answers, source links and visible editorial status.
  • Organization, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Product and Article JSON-LD aligned with the visible text.
  • Product filtering and article search are progressive enhancements; all records remain in the HTML.

Content governance / 内容治理

This is an owner-private redesign preview with noindex and crawl blocking. Public discovery requires production publication and a deliberate indexing switch. New process guides and articles are editorial drafts, not reviewed technical instructions. No author, reviewer, certificate, accuracy figure, price, stock level or customer result is fabricated.

Six representative products are included. The complete original catalogue, existing news articles, historical company pages and downloadable files still need a verified migration. Product tiles intentionally use model typography because original equipment images could not be retrieved reliably.

Production content model / 上线内容字段

ProductsOfficial model, category, process relationships, manufacturer-approved description, specifications with units and conditions, revisioned datasheet, installation limits, verification source and review date.
ArticlesTitle, explicit question, short answer, process relationship, sourced body, author identity, technical reviewer, publication date, revision date, references and related products.
ProcessesDefinition, applicable materials, outcomes, variables, scope limits, approved product mappings, FAQs and references.

Launch actions / 正式上线前

  1. Have Gauss approve the English terminology, product facts and process-to-product associations; complete the catalogue and article migration.
  2. Replace concept branding and typographic product tiles with approved brand assets and equipment photography. Hero is an AI-generated concept, not a picture of Gauss equipment.
  3. Assign real authors and technical reviewers. Publish only reviewed content and real, permissioned case evidence.
  4. Deploy on the intended domain; update canonical URLs and sitemap, remove preview noindex and crawler blocking, verify all redirects from legacy URLs and submit the sitemap.
  5. Connect a production enquiry service if web submission is desired. The current form opens an email draft.
  6. Measure qualified enquiries and organic landing-page performance; sample AI citations periodically without promising rankings.
