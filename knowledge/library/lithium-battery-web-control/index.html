Lithium-Ion Battery Web Handling: Tension Control and Web-Guiding Sensors

Quick answer

The coating, rolling, slitting, and winding of pole pieces in lithium battery manufacturing are all continuous coil processing processes. Tension fluctuations can cause pole pieces to stretch, wrinkle, break, and have uneven thickness. Edge deviation can cause poor alignment and safety hazards. The tension sensor and tension amplifier form the measurement end of the tension closed loop, and the infrared or photoelectric edge-grabbing electric eye forms the detection end of the correction system. The two work together to improve process stability and yield.

  • Coil process: coating, rolling, slitting, winding, lamination pre-processing
  • Unstable tension → stretching, wrinkles, broken belts, uneven thickness
  • Misalignment → Poor alignment, insufficient diaphragm coverage, safety risk
  • Tension amplifier: amplifies the tension sensor signal for closed-loop control
  • Edge-grabbing electric eye: detects the edge position of the web and drives the correction mechanism

Why lithium batteries require precision web control

The positive and negative electrode sheets and separators of lithium batteries are very thin continuous rolls. During the manufacturing process:

  • coating: The substrate runs at high speed in the oven, and the tension affects the uniformity of the coating;
  • Rolling: Tension and centering affect compaction density and pole piece flatness;
  • Cut: Unstable tension causes burrs and edge waves;
  • winding: The pole piece and separator must be accurately aligned. Deviation will cause the negative electrode to not completely cover the positive electrode, causing safety hazards.

Gauss Control's lithium battery industry page positions infrared edge-grabbing electric eyes and tension control sensors as components that improve the reliability and stability of the manufacturing process. The goal is to simplify procedures, reduce waste, and save time and costs.

Tension control

composition: Tension sensor (measuring roller force) → Tension amplifier (signal amplification and conditioning) → Controller → Actuator (magnetic powder brake, servo motor, etc.)

Consequences of unstable tension:

Tension state Consequence
Too big The pole piece is stretched, broken, and the coating is cracked
too small Wrinkles, loose curls, deviations
fluctuation Uneven thickness and density, uneven winding tightness

Key points for selection: The measuring range matches the web tension, signal stability and anti-interference, response speed, and is compatible with the control system interface.

Correction control (edge grabbing)

composition: Edge sensor (infrared/photoelectric edge sensor, ultrasonic, etc.) → Correction controller → Correction actuator (guide roller, moving frame)

Key points for selecting edge-grabbing electric eyes:

  • The detection materials (copper foil, aluminum foil, coated pole pieces, transparent separators) match the detection principle;
  • Detection accuracy and response speed meet linear speed;
  • Installation space and anti-pollution ability.

Integration with production data

Integrating tension, correction deviation and other data into the monitoring and traceability system can:

  • Correlate coil batches with process parameters;
  • Discover equipment abnormal trends;
  • Analyze the relationship between defects and process fluctuations.

Frequently asked questions

Can transparent diaphragms be inspected with infrared electric eyes?

Some transparent materials have high infrared transmittance and are difficult to detect. It is necessary to select a sensor or detection principle suitable for transparent materials. It should be tested and confirmed with actual materials.

Is open-loop or closed-loop tension control better?

For lithium battery processes that require high precision, closed-loop control with tension sensors is usually used.

Does the edge-grabbing sensor need to be calibrated frequently?

It is necessary to check the zero point and clean the detection window regularly, as dust and coating debris will affect the detection.
