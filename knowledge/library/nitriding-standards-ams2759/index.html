Nitriding and Nitriding Potential Control

Nitriding Standards: AMS2759/10, AMS2759/12, and Chinese National Standards

Quick answer

SAE AMS2759/10 (gas nitriding automatically controlled by nitriding potential) and AMS2759/12 (gas nitrocarburizing automatically controlled by nitriding potential and carbon potential) are commonly used in the aerospace field. They require nitriding potential rather than simple decomposition rate to control the process, and put forward requirements for measurement, control accuracy, recording and calibration. Commonly used in China are GB/T 18177 (gas nitriding of steel) and related standards for nitriding layer depth determination and metallographic inspection. The current version shall prevail for specific terms.

  • AMS2759/10: Gas nitriding, automatically controlled by nitriding potential
  • AMS2759/12: Gas nitrocarburizing, automatic control of nitriding potential and carbon potential
  • Common concerns: Kn measurement and control, temperature measurement (referenced to AMS2750), recording and traceability
  • Commonly used in China: GB/T 18177 and nitriding layer depth and metallographic inspection standards
  • The current version and customer-specific requirements shall prevail during implementation.

Why do aviation regulations emphasize nitriding potential?

Aerospace parts have strict requirements on the phase composition, compound layer thickness and fatigue performance of the nitrided layer. The traditional method of "controlling the decomposition rate" cannot guarantee consistent results under different furnace conditions, so SAE's AMS2759 series has specially formulated specifications with automatic control of nitriding potential (Kn) as the core.

AMS2759/10: Automatically controlled gas nitriding

Core idea:

  • by Nitriding potential As a process control parameter and automatically adjusted;
  • Put forward requirements for atmosphere measurement (such as hydrogen or ammonia analysis), control accuracy, and temperature uniformity;
  • Temperature measurement and furnace classification are often cited AMS2750 High temperature measurement specifications;
  • Complete process records, test specimens and inspection reports are required.

AMS2759/12: Automatically controlled gas nitrocarburizing

Adding the participation of carbon on the basis of AMS2759/10 requires automatic control of nitriding potential and carbon potential (or carbon activity related parameters), which is suitable for nitrocarburizing and optional post-oxidation treatment.

Common concerns when implementing specifications

  1. Measurement system: Accuracy, calibration cycle, and standard gas traceability of hydrogen probes or analyzers;
  2. control ability: Can the Kn deviation be controlled within the specifications or customer requirements;
  3. temperature system: TUS, SAT, thermocouple management;
  4. Record: The temperature, Kn, flow curve and alarm record of each furnace can be traced;
  5. sample: Testing methods and qualification criteria for furnace samples;
  6. People and documents: Operating procedures, training records, change management.

Implementation suggestions

  • First do a gap analysis: check existing equipment, instruments and documents one by one against the specifications;
  • Prioritize resolving measurement and recording issues, which are the most common non-conformities in audits;
  • Confirm with the customer if there are specific requirements (customer specifications may be higher than general standards);
  • The specifications are subject to revision, so be sure to use the latest version.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need to pay attention to AMS2759/10 even if you don’t do aviation parts?

It is not necessary, but it represents the advanced practice of controlled nitriding, and high-end customers in wind power, automobile and other industries often refer to its requirements.

Does AMS2759/10 specify a specific Kn value?

Specifications usually specify requirements for control methods, accuracy, and records. Specific Kn and temperature are determined by material and product specifications and customer drawings.

What are the main differences between national standard nitriding and AMS?

Mainly lies in the strictness of the control philosophy and recording requirements. The national standard stipulates more process and inspection, while AMS emphasizes process automatic control, instrument calibration and traceability.
