Specify it for your application

Share the furnace type, process atmosphere, existing instrumentation and production requirements. Gauss can confirm the configuration and technical limits for your installation.

  • Define the measured variables and the intended control task.
  • Confirm installation conditions, connections and maintenance access.

Where can I find the latest specifications?

Request the current datasheet from Gauss. Values such as measurement accuracy, operating limits and interface availability are configuration dependent and are not asserted on this page.

Can this product be integrated with my furnace?

A compatibility review is needed. Include the furnace model, existing controller and sensors, electrical drawings and process requirements in your enquiry.
